Courses & STEM Projects

1. Current Courses

We are currently offering the following courses:

AOPS+ Prealgebra

This online course builds on the renowned textbook Art of Problem Solving: Prealgebra, expertly preparing students for the rigors of algebra while teaching effective problem‑solving techniques to excel in competitive middle school math programs.

AOPS+ Algebra

This online course is based on the popular textbook Art of Problem Solving: Introduction to Algebra and concentrates on the algebra topics typically covered in Grades 6–9.
